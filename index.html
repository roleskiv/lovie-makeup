Our story

makeup that feels like a little love note

Lovie started with a simple idea: the things you use every day should be the things you love most. So we kept the line small, the shades soft and the packaging pretty enough to leave out on the shelf.

Every piece carries our four-point sparkle, a small reminder to give yourself a moment.
